pnpm task resume --all — rendered session FAKE DATA

Every loop id, change name and pid below is made up for illustration. The database is the repo config's .agentscript/task-loops.sqlite.

1. After a reboot: status marks interrupted loops

$ pnpm task status
14  add-plan-export      1.3        interrupted   2 iterations
13  add-plan-export      group 1    interrupted   1 iteration
12  add-plan-schema      2.1        blocked       3 iterations
11  add-resume-hint      group 2    interrupted   0 iterations
10  add-resume-hint      2.2        interrupted   1 iteration
 9  add-resume-hint      2.1        converged     2 iterations

Loop 12 is waiting for an answer and loop 9 has ended, so neither is marked. Loops 10 and 14 are task loops inside group loops 11 and 13.

2. Resume everything interrupted

$ pnpm task resume --all 2>progress.jsonl
loop 11 add-resume-hint group 2 (via 10) — converged
loop 13 add-plan-export group 1 (via 14) — blocked: agentscript-task answer 13 --db .agentscript/task-loops.sqlite --by principal --decision "…"
$ echo $?
1
$ head -3 progress.jsonl
{"event":"group-started","loop":11,"change":"add-resume-hint","group":"2","tasks":["2.2","2.3"],"at":"2026-10-13T09:02:11.000Z"}

{"event":"action","loop":10,"group":"2","iteration":2,"kind":"edit","summary":"…","refs":[]}

There is one line per outermost loop, in start order (11 started before 13). The nested task loops 10 and 14 carry on through their parents and are named after "via". They are not resumed twice.
